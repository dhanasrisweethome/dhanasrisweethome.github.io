About Us

Dhanasri Sweets & homefood is a family-run traditional sweet shop from Andhra Pradesh, known for its authentic handmade Pootharekulu. Every roll is crafted with care using pure ghee, fresh jaggery, and delicate rice starch sheets — preserving the true essence of our culture and taste.
